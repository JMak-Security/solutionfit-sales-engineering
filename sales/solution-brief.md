# QualityPath — fictional solution brief

> **Fictional product and illustrative scenario.** This brief is a portfolio demonstration, not a
> real product offer, customer case study, or guarantee.

## Hypothetical problem

Teams may spend time repeating manual regression checks across releases. Whether automation is
useful depends on the process, test stability, environments, coverage, implementation effort, and
ongoing costs.

## Hypothetical approach

QualityPath is a fictional QA automation product used to demonstrate a discovery-led evaluation.
The toolkit compares a manually entered baseline with a scenario that applies a visible automation
coverage assumption and adds license, support, and implementation costs.

## How to evaluate

1. Validate annual release volume and regression effort with the team.
2. Agree on a measurable baseline and exclusions.
3. Replace all illustrative cost inputs with reviewed values.
4. Test low/base/high assumptions and record technical dependencies.
5. Decide whether a bounded evaluation can measure the assumptions.

## Model boundaries

The included inputs do not describe a real organization or product. Automation coverage is not
measured. Annual workload is held constant. Monte Carlo samples independent triangular ranges; no
real-world correlation or probability is implied.
