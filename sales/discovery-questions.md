# Discovery question guide

> QualityPath is fictional. Use these questions to understand a prospect's stated process; do not
> imply that the example assumptions describe their organization.

## Current process

- How often do you release, and which teams participate in regression testing?
- Which test activities are manual today, and how are hours per release estimated?
- How do you currently track coverage, defects, and rework?
- Which constraints most affect release confidence: environment, data, skills, tooling, or time?

## Desired outcome

- What measurable outcome would make an evaluation worthwhile?
- Which existing metrics could validate that outcome?
- What should not change during a pilot?
- Who would validate technical fit, security, accessibility, and procurement requirements?

## Commercial assumptions

- Which cost categories belong in a comparison, and which should be excluded?
- What loaded labor rate and support effort are appropriate for your model?
- How should implementation effort and licensing be validated?
- Which assumptions should be tested as low/base/high scenarios?

## Decision and next step

- What evidence is needed before a decision?
- What is the evaluation timeline and who owns each action?
- Which risks or dependencies could stop the evaluation?
- Would a small, time-bounded technical validation be useful before any proposal?
